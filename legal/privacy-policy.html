Privacy policy

How China Pins Factory Co., Ltd. collects, uses, stores and shares personal data when you visit this website or send us an enquiry.

This policy explains what we do with the information you give us. It is written plainly, because a privacy policy nobody can read is not a privacy policy. It covers this website specifically. It does not cover how a third-party platform you visit from a link here handles your data.

Who we are

China Pins Factory Co., Ltd. is a manufacturer of custom lapel pins and metal badges, located in Kunshan, Jiangsu, China. We are the data controller for the information described in this policy.

If you have a question about this policy or about the data we hold about you, contact us at sales@chinapinsfactory.com or by telephone on +86-157-1104-7494.

What we collect

Information you give us

When you submit an enquiry we collect what you type into the form: your name and email address, the production process, quantity and size you are interested in, and anything else you write in the message field, including your company name, destination country, target price range and expected delivery date. If you attach or reference design artwork, we receive that too.

We ask for a target price range as an optional field. It is used to speed up the quotation, not as the basis for it, and it is never shared outside our sales process.

Enquiry forms on this website submit through Web3Forms, a form delivery service that forwards what you typed to our export desk inbox. It acts as a technical intermediary on our instructions: it receives the fields you submit and passes them on, and it is not permitted to use them for its own purposes. See who we share it with and how long we keep it.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider records standard server logs, which may include your IP address, the page requested, the referring page, the browser type and the time of the request. These records are used to keep the site running and to detect abuse. We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell visitor data to anyone.

Cookies and local storage

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies. It stores one item in your browser's local storage, the name of the preview theme you selected, so the page renders in the same style when you return. It contains no personal information and it never leaves your browser. Clearing your browser storage removes it.

Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To prepare and send your quotation, and to answer your questions about our products and production. This is our legitimate interest in responding to a business enquiry you initiated.
  • To perform a contract once an order is confirmed, including production, inspection, packing, shipping and export documentation.
  • To meet legal obligations, including export records, customs documentation and tax accounting.
  • To protect against fraud and to secure our systems.

We do not sell your personal data, we do not share it with advertising networks, and we do not use your enquiry to add you to a marketing mailing list without asking separately.

Who we share it with

We share only what is necessary to fulfil your order or deliver your enquiry:

  • Shipping carriers such as DHL, FedEx and UPS, who receive the contact details and destination needed to deliver the goods.
  • Customs authorities and freight forwarders, who receive the commercial documentation required to export and import the goods.
  • Professional advisers and auditors, including our accountant and any certification body auditing our quality management system.
  • Our hosting and email providers, who process data on our instructions under a written agreement.
  • Web3Forms, the form delivery service that transmits enquiry submissions to our inbox on our instructions. It stores a copy of the submission for up to three years by default, which is the retention period we ask it to apply to this site.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data to third parties for their own commercial use. If we ever needed to transfer data outside China to process your order, we would do so under the appropriate legal mechanism for cross-border transfer.

How long we keep it

Enquiry records that do not lead to an order are kept for up to twenty-four months so we can pick up a conversation where it left off, then deleted. The form delivery service that transmits submissions holds its own copy for up to three years unless we set a shorter retention period for this site, which we do. Data relating to a completed order is kept for the period required by tax and customs record-keeping obligations, which is typically seven years. Sample artwork and design files are kept for as long as you instruct us to, so we can support repeat orders.

Your rights

Depending on where you are based, you may have the right to ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict how we use it, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to receive it in a portable format. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time.

Write to sales@chinapinsfactory.com and we will respond. We answer requests within thirty days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Enquiry details are transmitted over HTTPS and stored on access-controlled systems inside our workshop. Production tooling and design files are held in the tooling shop with physical access control. We do not keep payment card details on file: card payments, where used, are processed by the payment provider and we receive only a confirmation.

No system is perfectly secure, but a breach of this kind is detectable, reportable and actionable, and we treat it as such.

Cookies on this site

The only storage this site uses is the theme preference described above. We do not run Google Analytics or any advertising tag, and we do not embed third-party trackers. If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy before it goes live and explain what it measures.

This site links to our Facebook and Instagram pages. Those links are ordinary outbound links: nothing is loaded from either platform while you are reading this site, and no information about your visit is sent to them. If you follow a link and then sign in to Facebook or Instagram, that platform applies its own privacy policy and cookie settings to your activity on it, which are outside our control. We do not use their pixels, embedded posts or social login widgets on this site.

Children

This site is a business website. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children, and our enquiry forms are intended for business buyers.

Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our practices change, and we change the date at the top when that happens. Material changes are also announced to existing contacts who have corresponded with us.
