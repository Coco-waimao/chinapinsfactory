Where these come from

Written after the order, not before it

Every guide here started as something a buyer got wrong and we had to fix. The line width page exists because pins came back with hairlines torn in the press. The process comparison exists because the same artwork was quoted four different ways. We write down what we learned, with the numbers attached.

  • Numbers from our own records, not from industry averages or a vendor brochure
  • Processes explained by what they are good at, not by a feature list
  • Mistakes covered too, because the ones buyers make most are the ones that cost money
  • No prices anywhere, since the right figure depends on your own specification
