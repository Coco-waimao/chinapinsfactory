Ordering and quantity

There is no minimum order quantity. You can order a single piece to check the finish and the clutch, a full set of samples across the plating range, or a production run. Volume pricing is applied against the quantity you order and confirmed in the quotation before you commit to anything.

Send a specification through the quotation form or by email. We reply with an itemised quotation and a 2D and 3D CAD proof inside 12 hours. You approve the proof, we confirm the sample if you want one, then production starts.

Vector files in AI, EPS or PDF are ideal, and a high resolution PNG works. A photograph of a physical badge or a rough hand sketch is genuinely enough to start, because twelve in-house designers vectorise from whatever you have, free of charge.

As many as you need before tooling is cut. Once the proof is approved the design is locked, because changing it afterwards means a new mould and a new charge. We would rather you iterate on the proof than on the metal.

We can reproduce a physical item from a photograph, or work from artwork you own. If the item carries a trademark, a logo of a third party or a protected character, it is your responsibility to hold the right to use it. We will happily point out when something looks copied from a competitor.

Specification and process

Hard enamel for the crispest raised metal line and the best price-to-look ratio, soft enamel for a glossy domed surface and fine detail, die struck for pure metal without colour, 3D die cast for genuine relief, and offset printed for flat artwork on a printed metal face. If you are unsure, describe the artwork and we will tell you which one we would use.

Pins from 14 mm to 60 mm in diameter, which is 0.55 in to 2.36 in. Challenge coins from 44 mm to 80 mm. Anything outside those ranges is possible, but it changes the tooling approach and we will say so before quoting.

Up to four Pantone references on a standard pin. Above that the enamel becomes fragile and the flatness of the surface suffers, so the honest answer is that more colours means a different product rather than a better one.

Brass and iron for hard enamel and die struck work, zinc alloy for 3D die casting, and stainless steel for plated pieces that need to resist corrosion. The base metal is chosen for the process and the finish, not to save money, and we tell you which one we are using.

Twelve: shiny 24K gold, matte 24K gold, antique gold, silver, brushed silver, black nickel, matte black nickel, rose gold, copper, antique brass, antique silver and matte ruthenium. Antique finishes recess the design in a darker tone and leave the high points bright, which is how most heritage and military work is meant to look.

Butterfly clutch as the standard, safety pin, screw back, magnet, and a flat or safety pin for lightweight printed pieces. On badges we also do a single pin, a brooch or a clutch depending on how the piece will be worn.

Samples and prototyping

Yes, and we would rather you did. A sample is the fastest way to settle plating, colour and clutch, because a flat render does not show how a finish catches light.

It is quoted rather than published, because it depends on the process, the size and whether a mould has to be cut. The sample cost is credited against the production order when you proceed, so you are not paying twice for the same piece.

A single piece in an existing finish is usually produced and shipped within a few business days. A sample needing a new mould takes longer, because the tool has to be cut first. Both timelines are confirmed in the quotation.

Weight in the hand, how the edge feels, how deep the relief actually is, and whether the colour reads the way you expected against your own artwork. A render shows shape and colour placement; only a physical sample shows finish and weight.

Pricing and payment

We quote rather than publish prices. Every pin differs in size, process, base metal, plating and colour count, and a single number cannot describe that honestly. The quotation itemises tooling, production, plating, packaging and freight so you can see what each part contributes.

USD for most enquiries, with EUR, GBP, AUD and CAD available on request. The invoice currency matches the quotation currency so there is no conversion surprise at the end.

Normally a deposit with the order and the balance against the shipping documents, or against a copy of the final inspection report if you prefer to pay before dispatch. We agree the schedule in the quotation rather than imposing one.

The tooling is quoted as a separate line. If you proceed to production it is a one-off cost for a mould that is yours to keep and reuse for repeat orders, which is why repeat runs of the same design do not pay it again.

Production and delivery

Seven to ten business days on our own floor after the sample is approved, plus shipping. A five business day rush is possible where the production window allows, and we confirm whether it does when we quote.

Quantity band, the process itself, and the number of separate sizes or designs in the order. A single design at one size runs faster than the same total split across six sizes, because each size needs its own tooling and setup.

Single polybag as the default, blister on a backing card, header bag with your own printed card and barcode, and gift box. Packaging is quoted separately because it is a real cost and a real decision.

FOB, CIF and DDP are all normal for us. FOB leaves you taking the container at our port, CIF includes freight and insurance to your destination port, and DDP delivers to your door with the import clearance handled by us where the destination allows it.

You are, as the importer, unless we have agreed DDP. HTS codes are confirmed by you before shipment because the classification depends on the material and the use, not on us. We supply the commercial invoice, packing list and certificate of origin either way.

Quality and compliance

Every piece is inspected under daylight before it is packed, and the inspector's name goes on the batch record. We check plating coverage, colour against the Pantone reference, the clutch or backing, dimensions against the approved proof, and the artwork registration.

ISO 9001:2015 quality management and BSCI social responsibility audit certificates, RoHS declarations, and CPSIA, California Proposition 65, EU REACH and EN71-3 testing on request. Certificates are issued against the alloy and the finish actually shipped, not a generic file.

Yes. XRF spectrometry runs on every plated batch before it leaves the workshop, so lead and cadmium content is verified by measurement rather than by paperwork. For EN71-3 and CPSIA we test the actual production batch on request.
