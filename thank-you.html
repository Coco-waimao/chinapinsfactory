Your enquiry is with our export desk in Kunshan. There is nothing else you need to do, and there is no obligation on either side until you approve a proof.

What happens next

Three things, in this order

A person reads every enquiry. Nothing here is an autoresponder, and nothing is passed to a sales agency.

We check the specification

An export desk engineer reads the process, size, quantity, finish and deadline you sent, and checks them against the current production window on our floor. If something in it does not work, we say so instead of quoting around it.

You get a proof and a quotation

Inside 12 hours, Monday to Saturday, you receive a flat artwork proof, a rendered 3D view of the piece in the finish you selected, and an itemised quotation that names every step it prices.

Nothing is charged yet

The proof and the quotation are free. You are not committed to tooling, to a minimum quantity or to an order until you approve both in writing.

Need us before the twelve hours are up?

If the programme is already in progress, or there is a hard deadline, message us directly and it comes to the front of the queue.
