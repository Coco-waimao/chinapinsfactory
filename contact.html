Who to ask for

Route it to the right desk

One inbox handles everything, but these are the questions each route answers fastest.

  • New enquiry goes to the export desk, which replies with a specification summary, a CAD proof and a quotation
  • Sample request is handled by the same desk, with the finish and size written into the sample order
  • An order already in production is taken by the production coordinator who owns that job, not by a general inbox
  • Payment and account questions go to finance, who answer on the same working day
  • Partnership and distribution is handled by the management team, and answered directly rather than by a form
