The form

Five required fields, the rest optional

The fewer fields you have to fill in, the more likely you are to actually send it. Only five are required: three describe what you want made, and two tell us where to send the quotation.

If you used the spec builder or one of our process pages, the details are carried over into this form automatically. If you would rather talk first, WhatsApp and email are answered Monday to Saturday.

Why there is no price on this page

Published prices invite buyers to compare a fraction of a cent while ignoring the finish, the colour count and the delivery terms. It also puts a floor under every negotiation you will ever have. We quote instead, and we put the information you actually need into the quotation instead.

  • An itemised quotation covering tooling, production, plating, packaging and freight
  • A 2D and 3D CAD proof of your design before any steel is cut
  • A written production schedule with the delivery date for your run
  • Volume rates applied automatically against the quantity you order

What happens next

From enquiry to shipment

A standard order follows this path. The bulk order guide covers each stage in more detail, including the trade terms and the customs side.

Within 12 hours

We read the specification and check it against capacity

Process, size, quantity, finish and delivery date are reviewed against the current production window. If something in the specification does not work, we tell you what the options are rather than quoting something that will fail later.

Within 12 hours

You receive a 2D and 3D CAD proof and an itemised quotation

The proof shows the exact pin in the exact finish. The quotation breaks down tooling, production, plating, packaging and freight so you can see what you are paying for. Nothing is cut before you approve it.

3 to 5 business days

A physical sample is produced

You check the plating, the enamel colour, the metal finish and the clutch in your hand. Adjustments are made from your feedback. The sample cost is credited against the production order.

7 to 10 business days

Production, inspection and packing

The run is produced, inspected piece by piece under daylight magnifiers, and packed to the specification you chose. A 5 business days rush is possible when a production window allows it.

On dispatch

Shipment with the documents you need

Dispatch by DHL, FedEx or UPS, with the commercial invoice, packing list and certificate documents you need for import. Blind drop-shipping to your warehouse or a fulfilment partner is supported.
