Choosing a process

The process decides more than the price

Metal thickness, relief depth, colour count and lead time all follow from the process you choose. Two pins that look identical in a photograph can be very different products, because one is lapped flat and the other is cast in relief.

  • Hard enamel for a flat, glossy, logo-forward pin with up to four Pantone references
  • Soft enamel when you need brighter colour and up to six references
  • Die struck when the pin must be all metal and nothing may chip
  • 3D die cast when the design has depth, cutouts or moving parts
  • Offset printed when the artwork has gradients or photography and the lead time is short
