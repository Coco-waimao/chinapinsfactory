Metal badges · Identification

Badges for organisations that issue them

A lapel pin is worn by one person for one programme. A badge is issued by an organisation, often in numbered sets, often with names on them, and often replaced every year. That difference changes how it should be made.

Badge families

Six shapes cover almost every use

The shape of a badge is set by what the organisation already issues. Send us an example and we will reproduce it, or we will recommend the closest standard form.

01

Round service badge

The general service and membership badge. A raised emblem with text around the rim, carried by clubs, associations and volunteer groups.

  • Diameter20 mm to 60 mm
  • ProcessHard enamel, soft enamel, die struck, 3D die cast
  • ColourUp to 4 Pantone references
  • FixingButterfly clutch, safety pin, screw back, magnet
02

Shield badge

Carries the authority of the issuing body. Used by security departments, event organisers, schools and sports governing bodies.

  • Height28 mm to 45 mm
  • ProcessHard enamel over a die struck shield, 3D relief for crests
  • Depth1.2 mm to 2.0 mm
  • FixingButterfly clutch or brooch
03

Rectangular name badge

A functional identification plate carrying a name, role or number. Usually issued in sets, often with a photograph panel, often replaced annually.

  • Size55 mm to 70 mm by 20 mm to 30 mm
  • ProcessOffset printed or die struck, colour-filled lettering
  • PersonalisationEngraved names and sequential numbers
  • FixingMagnet, safety pin or adhesive
04

Enamel organisation badge

A logo badge for staff, contractors and members of a company or institution. Cheaper than a full service badge and usually produced in larger sets.

  • Diameter16 mm to 35 mm
  • ProcessHard enamel or offset printed on a metal disc
  • MaterialBrass, iron or stainless steel
  • FinishAny of twelve plating options
05

Military and heritage badge

A subdued, antique-finished badge in the tradition of unit insignia. Antique plating recesses the design and leaves the high points bright, which is what makes the relief read.

  • Size28 mm to 45 mm
  • ProcessDie struck with 3D relief crest
  • FinishAntique gold, antique silver, matte black
  • AttachmentPin or clutch, pin back machined flat
06

Rubber and PVC badge

Soft, colourful and weatherproof, for events, campaigns and outdoor use where a metal badge would be too heavy or too easy to scratch.

  • Size20 mm to 50 mm
  • MaterialSoft PVC or rubber, moulded
  • ColourFull colour, no Pantone limit
  • FixingPin, magnet or key ring
Badge or lapel pin

The same factory, a different specification logic

The processes overlap, but the way a badge is specified is not the way a lapel pin is specified, and confusing the two leads to an expensive revision.

Comparison between custom metal badges and custom lapel pins
Point Metal badge Lapel pin
Typical size 20 mm to 60 mm, often rectangular 14 mm to 60 mm, almost always round
Text on the face Names, roles and numbers are common Short text only, and often curved
Personalisation Engraved names and sequential numbering Not usually personalised
Attachment Magnet, screw back or brooch is common Butterfly clutch is the standard
Order pattern Sets, often replaced on a yearly cycle One programme, one order
Repeat tooling The mould is reused every year, so it is worth specifying carefully Usually a one-off programme
Ordering badges

What we need to quote a badge run

Send what you have. If you have an existing badge, a photograph is more useful than a description, and if you do not have artwork at all we can work from a logo file and tell you what shape will carry it best.

  • The design, as artwork, a photograph of an existing badge, or a logo file
  • The shape and size, or the closest existing example you can send us
  • The quantity, including how many different names or numbers are involved
  • The finish, from the twelve plating options, and the colour count
  • The fixing, and whether the badge is worn, carried or displayed
  • The deadline, and whether it is a fixed event or a rolling replacement cycle
